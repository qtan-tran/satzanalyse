Sample corpus

Each sample sentence will come with a fully completed analysis frame: sentence functions, exact morphology, glosses, sense-group translations, verb table, translations, grammar notes and three pattern boxes (Example / Rule / Recognition tip) as a model of the method. How to study with it: paste a sentence into the Tool, fill in your own frame, then open the sample to compare.
Coming in Part 3
The sample corpus is on the roadmap: John's Gospel and Xenophon (easy prose for beginners), fully analysed sentence by sentence as worked examples. Until then, the Tool and the Guide are fully usable, and the Vietnamese edition satzanalyse-vn shows the corpus format in action.
