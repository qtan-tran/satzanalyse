Quick legend

14 sentence functions (standard colours used in every frame)
Each word block in the diagram has three layers: sentence function (top) – word (middle) – morphological form (bottom). A blank function cell (…) is where you assign the function after analysing. Reading a word block: the English gloss appears under the word when the dictionary finds it (the label "(ending stripped)" = guessed back to the dictionary – verify); the form cell: "?" = guessed from an ending (verify), "invariable" = does not decline (certain), "(form?)" = not guessed.
